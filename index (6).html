// ABOUT THE HOST

N.M. College of
Commerce & Economics

Established in 1946, Narsee Monjee College of Commerce & Economics (NM College) stands as one of Mumbai's most prestigious institutions. Part of the University of Mumbai, it has consistently ranked among the top colleges in Maharashtra.

Located in Vile Parle (West), Mumbai, NM College's tech community is hosting Byte Brainiacs — its first-ever 24-hour hackathon dedicated entirely to Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning innovation.

// FREQUENTLY_ASKED_QUESTIONS

Any undergraduate or postgraduate student from any college in India can participate. You don't need to be from a CS background — all we ask is that you're passionate about AI, ML, or tech in general. The hackathon is open to everyone aged 18+.

Bring your laptop, charger, college ID, and any hardware you plan to use. WiFi, power outlets, meals, and beverages will be provided. Also bring a valid government-issued photo ID for check-in verification.

No pre-built projects are allowed. All code must be written during the 24-hour event window. You may, however, prepare by studying APIs, frameworks, and tools beforehand. Existing open-source libraries can be used with proper attribution.

Projects are evaluated by a panel of industry mentors across four dimensions: Innovation & Creativity (30%), Technical Complexity (25%), Real-World Impact (25%), and Demo & Presentation Quality (20%). Each team gets 5 minutes to present and 3 minutes for Q&A.
